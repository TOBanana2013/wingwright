        Flying

        ↑↓ or WS nose up / down · ←→ or AD bank and turn (let go and the wings level out) · E / Q throttle up / down · 123 flaps: 1 Down for take-off and landing, 2 Flat switches on the cruise autopilot, 3 Up to go faster or come down · B airbrake · V cockpit / chase / side view · M map zoom · P or Esc pause · N sound on / off · R twice to restart. Each plane has a max height, where the engines fade out.

        Landing

        Follow the green route on the map, then keep the white line on the green one in the angle chart to glide down. 4 Landing prep slows you to landing speed and brings you down to the right height while you steer. Below 15 m, Left/Right swing the nose (rudder): in a crosswind, use them to point the nose straight down the runway just before touchdown. Touch down on the runway itself, lined up and with the wings level. On the ground, B brakes and Z reverse thrust is the emergency stop.

        Fast jets

        X afterburner: a big burst of thrust to pass Mach 1, burning fuel three times faster.

        Stunts

        The stunt plane can fly upside down: hold Nose up for a loop, hold Left or Right to roll, roll while climbing for a corkscrew. Upside down, nose up and nose down feel reversed. In the display team, stay in the green ring for group stunts.

        Fight

        Space fires the gun (it overheats if you hold it too long) · F fires a missile once the red box says LOCK · T picks the next target · G drops flares when a missile is coming. Bombers: Space drops bombs, 4 is the bomb run autopilot.

        Engine failure over the sea

        Glide at about 120 km/h and land gently on the water near the boat, or press J to bail out while you are at least 120 m up.
